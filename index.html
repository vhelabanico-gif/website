Our Story

About Us

Established in 2013 in Tacloban City, Leyte, we specialize in crafting memorable dining experiences for all occasions. Our business combines local culinary traditions with modern cooking techniques that brings delight to every plate.

We are committed to using fresh, locally-sourced ingredients and providing exceptional service tailored to your needs. Whether it's an intimate gathering or a grand celebration, we bring the finest Filipino flavors to your table.

Client Satisfaction

Vision

To be one of the trusted catering choice in Leyte and across the Philippines, celebrated for bringing Filipino flavors and warm hospitality to every gathering—from intimate family fiestas to large community events—while uplifting local farmers and craftsmen and making exceptional food accessible to all.

Mission

We are committed to delivering exceptional catering services by:
  • Preparing delicious, thoughtfully crafted dishes using fresh, locally-sourced ingredients whenever possible.
  • Providing personalized solutions tailored to each client's unique needs, budget, and cultural preferences.
  • Maintaining the highest standards of food safety, hygiene, and professional service.
  • Building lasting relationships with clients, partners, and communities through integrity and genuine care.
  • Minimizing our environmental footprint through responsible practices in sourcing, waste management, and operations.
